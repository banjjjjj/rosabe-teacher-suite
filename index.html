(25%)

Evidence & Support
(30%)

(25%)

(20%)
 Outstanding command of Grade 5 grammar, punctuation, and vocabulary. Good control of conventions with minimal non-distracting errors. Noticeable errors in grammar or spelling that occasionally distract. Frequent errors significantly obscure author meaning.
